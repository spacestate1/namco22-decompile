PROP CYCLE
==========

1. Put your propcycl.zip into the "roms" folder.
   (It is the MAME ROM set for Prop Cycle. Don't unzip it.)

2. Double-click PropCycle.exe.

That's it. The first start takes a few seconds while it unpacks the ROMs.


KEYS
----
  5             insert a coin
  Enter         start
  Arrow keys    steer and lean
  Esc           menu  (File > Restart or Exit, volume, controls, levels)
  F12           screenshot

A game controller works too.


PROBLEMS?
---------
"Prop Cycle needs its ROMs"     propcycl.zip is not in the roms folder, or
                                it is the wrong game. You need the MAME set
                                called "propcycl" (World, PR2 Ver.A).

"Windows protected your PC"     Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
                                (The game is not signed.)

Black screen or won't start     Update your graphics driver.

Needs Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
