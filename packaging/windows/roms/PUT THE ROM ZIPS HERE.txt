Put the MAME ROM sets in this folder, still zipped:

  Prop Cycle   propcycl.zip
  Rave Racer   raverace.zip  and  namcoc74.zip  (the sound chip's BIOS)
  Tokyo Wars   tokyowar.zip
  Dirt Dash    dirtdash.zip

Rave Racer, Tokyo Wars and Dirt Dash also need c71.bin (the DSP BIOS). If a game
says "c71.bin is missing", put MAME's namcoc71.zip in this folder too.
